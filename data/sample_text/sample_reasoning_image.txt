Questions 1-3

1. A recent study found that employees who work remotely report higher job satisfaction than employees who work entirely in offices. However, the study surveyed only employees at technology companies.

(A) Technology companies are more likely than other companies to offer flexible schedules.
(B) Many employees prefer working in offices to working remotely.
(C) Some technology companies require employees to work on-site.
(D) Job satisfaction is difficult to measure objectively.
(E) Remote workers often save money on commuting.

2. Which one of the following, if true, most weakens the argument?

(A) Employees at non-technology companies who work remotely report lower job satisfaction than those at technology companies.
(B) Remote workers are generally less productive than office workers.
(C) Technology companies pay higher salaries than most other companies.
(D) Office workers tend to collaborate more often in person.
(E) Many employees value workplace amenities.

3. The argument depends on assuming which one of the following?

(A) Job satisfaction is unrelated to salary.
(B) Employees at technology companies are representative of employees generally.
(C) Office workers never work from home.
(D) Technology companies have better managers than other companies.
(E) Remote work always increases productivity.
